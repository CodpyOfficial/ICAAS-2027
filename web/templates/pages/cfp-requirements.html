Paper format

  • Prepare the paper with the IEEE conference template: Microsoft Word (US letter or A4) or LaTeX, also available on Overleaf. Remove the template's guidance text.
  • There is no page limit.
  • Submit one PDF of at most {{maxPaperMB}} MB, anonymized for the double-blind review.
  • Accepted papers are submitted again as camera-ready papers in the same format; see the important dates.

Keeping the review double-blind

  • Do not put author names, affiliations or self-identifying links in the title, abstract or outline.
  • Refer to your own prior work in the third person.
  • Author details, bio and project links are entered in a separate section of the submission form.

Demo video tips (tutorials)

  • Keep it between 2 and 5 minutes and show the working prototype, the workflow or the outcome participants will build.
  • Use a publicly viewable or unlisted link (YouTube, Vimeo, Loom or Google Drive) and check that it opens without signing in.
