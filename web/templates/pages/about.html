The program

  • {{icon "laptop"}}Tutorials — a 5-minute presentation in the morning and a 90-minute workshop for 25 or 50 participants in the afternoon, assisted by teaching assistants.
  • {{icon "book"}}Regular papers — 10-minute talks in the late morning, after the tutorial presentations, and posters in the afternoon, during the coffee break.
  • {{icon "mic"}}Two keynotes by leaders in autonomous systems, at the end of the afternoon.
  • {{icon "briefcase"}}Industry exhibition with companies building autonomous systems.

Conference theme

The theme spans the full autonomy stack and puts implementation first: robotics, vehicles, drones, embedded computing, perception, simulation and applications lead the list. Submissions aligned with these areas are warmly welcomed, though not strictly limited to them.

Why ICASW?

For authors

Tutorial authors turn their work into an experience participants can reproduce, and receive 40% of the workshop registration fees as feedback. Regular paper authors present to an audience that builds real systems.

For participants

Learn the latest research, then sit down with the people who built the tools and try them yourself, guided step by step through milestone-based workshops.

Organization

{{$c.FullTitle}} is organized by the {{$c.Acronym}} Organizing Committee. Proposals are evaluated by the Technical Program Committee through double-blind peer review.
