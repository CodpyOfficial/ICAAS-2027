Format & schedule

Each regular paper is presented twice: in a 10-minute talk in the late morning, after the tutorial presentations, and as a poster in the afternoon, during the coffee break while the tutorial workshops take place. The poster size and format will be announced to accepted authors.

Topic scope

Submissions aligned with the conference theme are warmly welcomed, though topics are not strictly limited to it — any compelling, relevant research is welcome.
