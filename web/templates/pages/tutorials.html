Each tutorial has two parts: a 25-minute presentation in the morning, then a 90-minute workshop in the afternoon in which its authors lead 25 or 50 participants through hands-on exercises, assisted by at least one teaching assistant. The workshops run in parallel. Participants bring their own laptops, meeting the hardware requirements published for each workshop; the authors prepare the equipment and environment.
