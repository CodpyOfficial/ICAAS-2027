Conference web app

This website works as an app on your phone: add it to your home screen to reach the program and news in one tap during the conference.

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Android (Chrome): open the browser menu, then “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
