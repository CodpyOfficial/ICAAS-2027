Each tutorial includes a 90-minute afternoon workshop for 25 or 50 participants. Workshop seats are booked separately and are limited, so register early for the workshops you want.

How it works

  1. Register for the conference. See the registration fees.
  2. Choose your workshops. Book a seat in the workshops you want to join; seats are allocated in order of registration.
  3. Prepare your laptop. Make sure it meets the workshop's laptop hardware requirements, and follow the installation guide the authors send before the conference.
  4. Join the morning presentation. The authors’ 25-minute tutorial presentation in the morning introduces what you will build.
