{{$c.FullTitle}} is committed to a professional, inclusive and safe environment for everyone, at the conference and online.

Code of conduct

All participants — attendees, authors, keynote speakers, workshop instructors, exhibitors, organizers and volunteers — are expected to:

  • Treat others with respect and consideration, and engage in critique of ideas rather than people.
  • Refrain from harassment, intimidation and discrimination in any form, including unwelcome comments, imagery or physical contact.
  • Respect the safety instructions of the venue and of workshop instructors, in particular when working with robots, vehicles or other hardware.
  • Ask before photographing or recording people, and follow the presenters' wishes about recording their talks and workshops.

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behaviour is expected to comply immediately. The organizers may take any action they deem appropriate, including removal from the conference without refund.

Ethics and reporting

Proposals must be the original work of their authors. Plagiarism, fabrication of results and attempts to break the anonymity of double-blind review are not acceptable. To report a concern about conduct or research ethics, contact the organizers through the contact form; reports are handled confidentially.

Non-discrimination

{{$c.Acronym}} welcomes participants regardless of gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age or career stage.

Accessibility

This website is built to be usable with a keyboard and screen readers, to respect reduced-motion settings, and to work on phones and desktops. If you need assistance to take part in the conference, or find part of this site hard to use, please let us know.

Privacy policy

This website collects personal data only through its forms:

  • Proposal submissions, including the uploaded PDF papers, are stored on the conference server and used solely to run the review and build the program. Author details are kept separate from the anonymous parts of the proposal during double-blind review.
  • Contact messages are used to answer your enquiry.
  • Newsletter subscriptions are used to send conference updates; ask us at any time to remove your address.

The site sets a single technical cookie that protects forms against cross-site request forgery; it contains no personal data and is not used for tracking. There are no advertising or analytics cookies. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. To access, correct or delete your data, contact the organizers.

Terms of use

The content of this website is provided for information about {{$c.FullTitle}} and may change without notice; the dates and details published here prevail over earlier announcements. Logos and promotional material may be used to promote the conference as described on the Downloads page.
