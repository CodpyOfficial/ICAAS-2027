The authors of each paper with workshop lead a 90-minute workshop in the afternoon, following their 25-minute morning presentation. Each workshop is for 25 or 50 participants and is assisted by at least one teaching assistant. Participants bring their own laptops, meeting the hardware requirements published for each workshop; the authors prepare the equipment and environment.
