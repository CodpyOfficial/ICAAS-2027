Invitation process

  1. Selection. Guest editors select contributions based on quality, timeliness and the review outcome.
  2. Invitation. Invited authors receive instructions for submitting an extended manuscript.
  3. Review. Extended manuscripts go through the journal's own peer review.

Important. A journal version must substantially extend the conference contribution with new, non-trivial material.

Workshop materials

Tutorial authors are encouraged to publish their workshop material (code, environment and instructions) in a public repository, so participants can revisit it after the conference.
