Morning presentation (25 minutes)

  • Prepare your slides with the official ICASW slide template (mandatory).
  • Introduce your work and show what participants will build in the afternoon.
  • Follow the breakdown you gave in your proposal and keep time for questions.

Afternoon workshop (90 minutes)

  • The workshop is for 25 or 50 participants, as chosen in your proposal. If it needs equipment, prepare 10 sets for 25 participants or 20 sets for 50 participants.
  • You are responsible for preparing all necessary equipment and environment set-ups; attendees bring their own laptops.
  • Provide the laptop hardware requirements (CPU, memory, GPU, disk space, operating system) for participants; they are published on the workshop pages.
  • Bring at least one teaching assistant (TA) to help participants with set-up issues and the practical exercises.
  • Send participants installation instructions in advance, and bring offline copies of large downloads, containers or images.
  • Organize the session around clear milestones so that everyone reaches a working result, and plan a fallback for participants whose set-up fails.

Regular papers

Each paper is allocated an estimated 15 minutes, in the late morning after the tutorial presentations.

Preparing your slides

  • Use the official ICASW slide template (mandatory), with fonts of 18 pt or larger and high-contrast figures.
  • Keep each slide focused: a few words per slide, one message per figure.
  • Embed fonts and bring a PDF backup on a USB stick.

On the day

  • Arrive at your session room at least 15 minutes before the session starts and introduce yourself to the session chair.
  • Load and test your slides during the break before your session.
  • Respect your time slot so the session stays on schedule before the lunch break.

Official slide template

The template contains the title, content, section, figure and closing slides. Duplicate the slides you need and replace the sample text; do not change the layout, colours, emblem or footer.
