The Technical Program Committee (TPC) evaluates every proposal in double-blind peer review and builds the technical program.

How the review works

  1. Assignment. Each proposal is assigned to reviewers with expertise in its topic and track.
  2. Double-blind review. Reviewers do not see who the authors are, and authors do not learn who reviewed them.
  3. Evaluation. Proposals are scored on technical depth & originality, clarity & feasibility, engagement plan and theme alignment.
  4. Selection. Track chairs select the 5 papers with workshop and 8 papers without workshop for the program.
