About

About {{$c.Acronym}}

The {{$c.Name}} ({{$c.Acronym}}) is a forum for researchers, engineers and practitioners working on the theory, design and deployment of systems that perceive, decide and act on their own — from robots and vehicles to self-adaptive software.

{{$c.FullTitle}} combines two complementary formats: tutorials, where authors present in the morning and run a 90-minute interactive workshop in the afternoon, and regular papers, presented as focused 10-minute talks and as posters.

  • {{icon "check"}}Tutorials: morning presentations and laptop-based afternoon workshops
  • {{icon "check"}}Regular paper talks in the late morning and posters in the afternoon
  • {{icon "check"}}Two keynote talks at the end of the afternoon
  • {{icon "check"}}Industry exhibition

The conference theme puts implementation first — autonomous robotics, vehicles, drones, embedded computing, perception, simulation and real-world applications — and extends to control, planning, learning, multi-agent systems, human–autonomy teaming, safety, security and the ethics and policy questions autonomy raises.

All proposals undergo double-blind peer review for technical depth and originality, clarity and feasibility, engagement, and alignment with current industry and academic trends.
