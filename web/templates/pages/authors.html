Proposal submission

  • Proposals are submitted online only through the proposal submission form; submissions by email are not accepted.
  • All submissions must be in English.
  • Indicate the track clearly: Tutorial or Regular Paper.
  • You receive a submission ID on the confirmation page; quote it in all correspondence.
  • To change a submitted proposal before the deadline, contact the organizers with your submission ID.

Detailed submission requirements

After acceptance

  1. Confirm and register. At least one author of each accepted proposal is expected to register by the registration deadline.
  2. Submit the camera-ready paper. Revise the paper following the reviews and submit the final version in the IEEE conference format by the camera-ready deadline on the important dates page. Instructions are sent with the notification of acceptance.
  3. Prepare your material. Prepare your slides with the official ICAAS slide template (mandatory) and follow the presentation guidelines; tutorial authors also prepare all workshop equipment and environment set-ups, provide the laptop hardware requirements for participants, and arrange at least one teaching assistant; regular paper authors also prepare a poster for the afternoon poster session.
  4. Present in person. Accepted contributions are presented at the conference.

Author feedback (tutorials)

Tutorial authors receive 40% of the registration fees of their workshop as feedback, based on attendance. The arrangements will be communicated to the accepted authors.
