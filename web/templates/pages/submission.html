Submit a paper with workshop or a paper without workshop

{{icon "info"}}

Before you start

  • Write everything in English.
  • Keep the title, abstract and outline anonymous — the review is double-blind.
  • Upload your paper as one PDF file (max {{maxPaperMB}} MB) in the IEEE conference format, without author names. There is no page limit.
  • Abstract: max 300 words. Author bio: max 150 words.
  • Accepted authors must present with the official ICASW slide template.
  • Papers with workshop also need the prerequisites & toolchain, the workshop size (25 or 50 participants), the laptop hardware requirements for participants and at least one teaching assistant.
  • {{icon "video"}} Papers with workshop must include a 2–5 minute demo video (public or unlisted link).
