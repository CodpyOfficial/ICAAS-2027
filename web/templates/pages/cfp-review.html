Review process

  1. Submission. Proposals are submitted online in English, with the track clearly indicated.
  2. Double-blind review. Reviewers evaluate the anonymous proposal; author information is kept separate.
  3. Workshop feasibility check. For tutorials, the demo video, milestone plan, equipment, laptop requirements and teaching assistants show whether the workshop can be completed in 90 minutes.
  4. Selection and notification. The program committee selects the {{(track "with-workshop").Slots}} tutorials and {{(track "without-workshop").Slots}} regular papers and notifies all submitters.
